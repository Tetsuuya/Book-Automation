# UNIVERSAL QUALITY ASSURANCE & CURRICULUM AUDIT STANDARDS
## DepEd MATATAG Zero-Hallucination, Pedagogical Rigor, and Grade-Level Readiness Framework

This document establishes the mandatory **Quality Assurance (QA) and Curriculum Audit Protocol** for all textbooks generated under the Department of Education (DepEd) **MATATAG K-10 Curriculum** across ALL disciplines (Science, Mathematics, English, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, TLE/TVL, and Values Education / GMRC) for Grades 1 through 10.

Every generated page, paragraph, formula, literary excerpt, historical date, and assessment item MUST pass this audit prior to publication.

---

# PILLAR 1: CURRICULUM ALIGNMENT & COMPETENCY INTEGRITY

Every unit must strictly demonstrate 100% fidelity to the official DepEd MATATAG Curriculum Guides:

1. **Explicit Competency Anchor in Section 1**:
   - Every Lesson MUST open with the official **MATATAG Competency Codes**, Content Standards, and Performance Standards.
   - Example: `Koda ng Kasanayan: F7PN-IIIa-c-13, F7PB-IIIa-c-14` or `Competency Code: S8FE-Ia-15, S8FE-Ia-16`.
2. **The 3 Essential Questions (Mahahalagang Tanong)**:
   - Must frame overarching inquiry, not trivial factual recall.
   - Each of the 3 subtopics in the lesson MUST explicitly answer one of the Essential Questions.
3. **Strict 70/30 Instructional Ratio & 45-70 Page Publication Target**:
   - **70% Instructional Depth**: Lessons must be masterclasses of deep, contextualized prose. Paced strictly by quarter scope:
     * *3-Lesson Units (Filipino, English, Values Ed, AP)*: 4,000–4,500 words per lesson floor (~18,000 words total across unit -> 48–55 pages in Word).
     * *5–6 Lesson Quarters (Mathematics, Science STEM)*: Calibrated to 2,200–2,600 words per lesson (6 lessons × 2,400 = 14.4k + 4.5k Back Matter = 18.9k words total -> 50–60 pages in Word, strictly <= 70 pages max). Summaries, bulleted outlines, and shorthand placeholders (`...`) are strictly prohibited.
   - **30% Formative Practice**: Guided practice, authentic contextual tasks, 15-item formative quizzes, and culminating quarter assessment.
   - **Strict 45 to 70 Page Publication Range**: The complete compiled publication (Front Matter, Lessons, Culminating Assessment, Synthesis, Glossary, and Structured Answer Keys) MUST land squarely between 45 and 70 pages in Microsoft Word.
4. **Spiral Progression Principle**:
   - Concepts must build systematically on prior grade competencies without regressing to elementary simplifications or making un-scaffolded leaps into collegiate theory.

# PILLAR 2: GRADE-LEVEL DEVELOPMENTAL & LEXICAL APPROPRIATENESS

The language, syntax, cognitive demand, and emotional themes must be precisely calibrated to the developmental stage of the learners:

### 1. Cognitive & Lexical Benchmarks by Grade Band:

| Grade Level | Age Band | Cognitive Benchmark (Bloom / DOK) | Lexical & Syntactic Standards | Key Subject Sensitivities |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 7** | 12–13 yrs | **Understanding & Application** (DOK 1–2). Transition from concrete to early abstract thinking. | Bridging familiar conversational language to formal academic register. Clear topic sentences, explicit transitional markers, lexile ~850L–1050L. | Focus on personal identity, peer relationships, local community ecosystems, basic laboratory safety, and foundational literary appreciation. |
| **Grade 8** | 13–14 yrs | **Analysis & Comparative Evaluation** (DOK 2–3). Examining underlying structures, perspectives, and biases. | Complex sentence structures, subordinate clauses, disciplinary terminology introduced with contextual glosses, lexile ~950L–1150L. | Critical analysis of colonial history, Asian geopolitical context, forces & motion dynamics, moral decision-making amidst social pressure. |
| **Grade 9** | 14–15 yrs | **Evaluation & Systemic Modeling** (DOK 3–4). Analyzing interconnected systems, formal proofs, and philosophical tensions. | Scholarly vocabulary, multi-clause arguments, rhetorical subtleties, domain-specific academic prose, lexile ~1050L–1250L. | Macroeconomics, chemical bonding & thermodynamics, quadratic modeling, world literature, ethics of social responsibility and civic rights. |
| **Grade 10** | 15–16 yrs | **Synthesis, Creation, & Authentic Inquiry** (DOK 4). Formulating original hypotheses, scholarly critique, and advocacy. | Pre-collegiate academic discourse, synthesizing multiple primary sources, nuanced technical exposition, lexile ~1150L–1350L. | Contemporary global and national issues, genetic technologies, trigonometric modeling, independent research proposals, ethical leadership. |

### 2. Socio-Emotional & Values Appropriateness (GMRC / Values Ed / EsP):
- Dilemmas must reflect authentic situations faced by modern Filipino adolescents (e.g., peer pressure, academic integrity, social media dynamics, family communication, financial stewardship).
- Avoid preaching, moralizing lectures, or exclusionary religious dogmatism. Focus on ethical reasoning, empathy (*kapuwa*), critical discernment (*mapanuring pagpapasiya*), and community welfare.

### 3. Gender-Fair & Inclusive Language (DepEd Order No. 32, s. 2017 & IPED Framework):
- **Gender Fairness**: Use gender-neutral terminology (e.g., *mga mag-aaral*, *kabataan*, *humanity*, *chairperson*). Ensure equal representation of female and male figures in scientific, historical, and leadership roles.
- **Cultural Inclusivity**: Positively and accurately reflect the rich diversity of Indigenous Peoples (IPs), Bangsamoro communities, and rural and urban Philippine contexts. Free of cultural stereotyping.

# PILLAR 3: ZERO-HALLUCINATION & FACTUAL ACCURACY PROTOCOLS

The AI must NEVER fabricate data, dates, laws, historical figures, or mathematical derivations. Adhere to these discipline-specific verification rules:

### A. FILIPINO & PANITIKAN (Wika at Panitikan)
- **Authentic Literary Selections**: Anchor texts MUST be genuine historical or recognized literary works (e.g., *Doctrina Christiana*, *Ibong Adarna*, *Florante at Laura*, *Noli Me Tangere*, *Urbana at Felisa*, mga tula nina Jose Corazon de Jesus, Amado V. Hernandez). Never invent fake folk tales or credit modern synthetic text to historical authors.
- **Metric & Poetic Rules**:
  * **Tanaga**: Exactly 4 lines, 7 syllables per line (7-7-7-7), with traditional monorime (AAAA) or modern rhyme schemes (AABB, ABAB). Count syllables (*pantig*) rigorously!
  * **Haiku**: Exactly 3 lines, 5-7-5 syllables.
  * **Awit**: 4 lines, 12 syllables per line, caesura (*hati*) after the 6th syllable.
  * **Korido**: 4 lines, 8 syllables per line, fast tempo (*allegro*).
- **Linguistic Standards**: Strictly observe the *Ortograpiyang Pambansa* of the Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino (KWF). Accurate identification of grammatical structures (e.g., pokus ng pandiwa, kaganapan, kohesyong gramatikal, pagpapalawak ng pangungusap).

### B. ENGLISH & LITERATURE
- **Authentic Literary Selections**: Feature verified selections from Philippine Literature in English (Nick Joaquin, Manuel Arguilla, Paz Marquez Benitez, Carlos Bulosan) and World Literature.
- **Literary Devices & Syntax**: Accurately explain and apply figures of speech, meter (iambic pentameter), narrative viewpoints, and grammatical syntax rules.

### C. ARALING PANLIPUNAN (History, Geography, & Economics)
- **Historical Accuracy**: Verified dates, treaties, and actors:
  * Tejeros Convention: March 22, 1897 (NOT 1896).
  * Proclamation of Independence in Kawit, Cavite: June 12, 1898.
  * Treaty of Paris: December 10, 1898.
  * First Philippine Republic Inauguration in Malolos: January 23, 1899.
- **Primary Source Integrity**: Verbatim fidelity when quoting historical documents (*Kartilya ng Katipunan* ni Emilio Jacinto, *Liwanag at Dilim* ni Emilio Jacinto, *El Verdadero Decalogo* ni Apolinario Mabini, Saligang Batas 1987).
- **Legal & Policy Citations**: Real Republic Acts only:
  * R.A. 10533: Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013.
  * R.A. 11476: GMRC and Values Education Act.
  * R.A. 9165: Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act.
  * R.A. 9003: Ecological Solid Waste Management Act.
  * **NEVER invent fictitious Republic Act numbers or executive orders!**

### D. SCIENCE (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth & Space)
- **Empirical Rigor**: Strict adherence to established scientific laws, SI units (kg, m, s, N, J, W, Pa), IUPAC chemical nomenclatures, balanced chemical reactions, and taxonomic hierarchies.
- **Reproducible Laboratory Protocols**: All experimental procedures in Section 4 must specify realistic, safe, and feasible materials (school lab or household items), explicit control variables, and standard safety precautions.

### E. MATHEMATICS
- **Zero Math Errors**: Every step in mathematical proofs, quadratic derivations, algebraic simplifications, and geometric theorems must be mathematically sound.
- **Calculation Verification**: Double-check all sample computations and numbers in word problems. Ensure numbers are realistic (e.g., a person cannot run at 120 km/h; dimensions of a classroom cannot be negative).
- **Answer Key Concordance**: The answers in the Susi sa Pagwawasto MUST mathematically match the questions in the 15-item quiz and 40-pt Unit Assessment.

# PILLAR 4: STRUCTURAL, VISUAL, & MULTIMEDIA COMPLIANCE

1. **Cover Typography & Title Banner**:
   - Deep Navy Blue banner at the top specifying 3 tiers of text in quotes: `"[BOOK TITLE] [SUBJECT] [GRADE]"`, `"UNIT [X]: [THEME]"`, and `"[Scope 1] • [Scope 2]"`.
   - Never use generic `[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]` on Page 1.
2. **Dedicated Visual Container Boxes & Strict Ban on Draft Labels**:
   - **BAN DRAFT LABELS**: Strict zero-tolerance for `"Visual Prompt 1"`, `"Visual Prompt 2"`, `"sample visual prompt"`, or internal notes in the document! Use ONLY production titles: `🖼️ Larawan: [Mapanuring Pamagat]` or `🖼️ Figure: [Descriptive Title]`.
   - **100% Boxed Containment**: Title, Pedagogical Purpose, AI Prompt, and Replacement Note MUST ALL be cleanly enclosed inside ONE dedicated container box (`> ...` in Markdown or 1-cell shaded table in DOCX). No loose text outside the box.
   - **Zero Branding in Image Prompts**: NO `"DepEd"`, `"MATATAG"`, or publisher logos inside AI image prompts. NO DepEd/MATATAG marks anywhere in the book either (cover, running headers, footers); the curriculum is cited only in lesson alignment lines and the sources table.
   - **Strict Image-Prompt Quota**: Exactly 1 Cover Prompt on Page 1 (`--ar 8.5:11`) + 3 Image Prompts per Lesson (`--ar 16:9`) = **1 + 3 × lessons** (10 for a 3-lesson unit, 19 for a 6-lesson quarter). Zero image prompts in Back Matter.
3. **Bulletproof YouTube Multimedia Modules (Topic-Specific Direct Video Links, Zero Fake Timestamps, Zero Disclaimers)**:
   - **Legitimate & Verified Media**: Video module must cite legitimate, active channels (*DepEd TV Official, Knowledge Channel, CrashCourse, Khan Academy, DOST-SEI, GMA Public Affairs, TED-Ed, SoulPancake*).
   - **Topic-Specific Direct Real Video Link**: Must provide the authentic direct video URL (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=[REAL_VIDEO_ID]`) specifically covering THAT lesson's topic or activity. **NO SEARCH LINKS** (`results?search_query=...`) and **NO GENERIC WHOLE-COURSE COMPILATIONS**.
   - **🚫 ZERO AI DISCLAIMERS OR EXCUSES**: Strict ban on publishing meta-excuses like *"The multimedia box intentionally does not invent a direct video address..."* or *"link should be inserted during publication verification"*. Always pull an authentic verified video from the **Curated Topic-Specific Video Bank** in `02_VISUAL_AND_MULTIMEDIA_STANDARDS.md`. Zero text outside the box.
   - **BAN HALLUCINATED TIMESTAMPS**: Never invent fake minutes and seconds (e.g. `02:15 - 04:30`). Instead, provide 2–3 **Key Concepts & Themes to Observe** in the video, followed by 3 Socratic Viewing Questions.
   - **Zero QR Codes**: Banned to prevent broken ASCII boxes.
4. **Editorial Purity & Lesson Content Fit (Zero Draft Residue)**:
   - Zero meta-commentary, conversational filler ("Sure, here is Lesson 1", "In this section we will"), or developer placeholders.
   - Prose must flow with seamless academic authority and continuity across all 10 lesson sections.
5. **Navy Header System & Non-Table Table of Contents**:
   - Heading 1 in solid Navy Blue (`#1B365D`) banner cell with white text.
   - Heading 2 in soft blue (`#EBF3FA`) block with 4.5pt solid Navy left border.
   - **Page 3 Table of Contents**: Formatted strictly as a hierarchical indented typographic list with right-aligned page numbers. **STRICTLY NO TABLE GRIDS** (`| ... |`)!
6. **Unit Assessment Architecture & Structured Table Keys**:
   - **Unit Assessment (50 Pts)**: Comprehensive 30-Item Multiple Choice Quiz (Items 1–30 with `[  ]` checkboxes, balanced A–D spread) followed immediately by the Authentic Performance Task (GRASPS Model + Complete 4x4 Analytic Rubric).
   - **Structured Table Answer Keys**: Rendered in clean table grids (10-col for lessons, two 15-col grids for 30-item Unit Assessment, plus 2-col rationale tables). **ZERO paragraph blobs!**
7. **Psychometric Option Balance & Anti-'Mostly B' Bias**:
   - Correct answers MUST be evenly distributed across options `A`, `B`, `C`, and `D` (~25% each; 2 to 3 per 10 items).
   - Strict ban on letter-clustering bias (never default to 'B' or any single letter).
   - Zero consecutive runs of >2 identical letters (e.g. `A-A-C` is allowed, `B-B-B` is prohibited).
   - **Zero predictable cycles**: A key must never follow a repeating pattern such as `A-B-C-D-A-B-C-D…` or `D-C-B-A…`, and no two quizzes in the same book may share an identical answer sequence. Shuffle option order per item, then re-verify the key against every item.
   - Parallel distractor length and plausibility so the correct option is not consistently the longest or most detailed.
